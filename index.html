Sign a PSBT

Base64 or hex. Every input needs a witness utxo. Inputs paying to this key's untweaked taproot output get signed. On the BLAKE2b chains the signature opts in to the unified sighash (0x21), on the SHA256d chains it is BIP341 default. A blocktrail update is just a PSBT here.
